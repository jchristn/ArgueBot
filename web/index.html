ArgueBot

Start a debate

Pick who's arguing, give them a topic, and watch them work toward consensus. You can step in between turns, or cut in at any time.

1. Who's debating?

Choose two or three agents. They speak in the order you select them.

2. What's the topic?

⌘/Ctrl + Enter to start

3. Settings

The same options as the terminal flags. Your choices are remembered in this browser; flags passed when starting the server (e.g. ./go-web.sh --rounds 8) take precedence.
